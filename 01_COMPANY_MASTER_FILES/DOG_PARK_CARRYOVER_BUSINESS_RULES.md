# Dog Park Carryover Business Rules

Dog Park is finished, but these rules must carry into the company:

## Colour rules

- Green: suitable based on available proof.
- Amber/yellow: caution, uncertain, check first, needs space, trace risk.
- Red: do not proceed, stop, blocked risk, unsafe.
- Black: emergency only, official direction or critical incident evidence.

Colour must always include words and action instructions.

## Safety wording

Use:
- decision support
- helps choose safer options
- check current conditions
- follow signs/professionals/emergency services

Avoid:
- guaranteed safe
- 100% safe
- replacing doctors, vets, lawyers, emergency services or food labels

## Privacy

- private fields hidden by default
- sharing must be optional
- emergency contacts only used with user control
- sensitive records stay private unless user chooses otherwise

## Accountability

- responsible person remains attached to the action
- logs and timestamps matter
- reports should be factual and non-defamatory

## Product separation

Carry over the safety logic and brand structure, not the Dog Park app code.
