# Cross-ecosystem link rules

1. Every product has one canonical family/home.
2. A site/customer/configuration does not become a new core merely because it has a new name.
3. Shared Core provides reusable contracts/engines, not a shared customer database.
4. Standalone products retain commercial/runtime/data boundaries while reusing approved generic capabilities.
5. Regulated, clinical, child-safeguarding, emergency, animal-clinical and tactical rules remain sector-owned.
6. Cross-product bridges require explicit purpose, source/version, minimum-necessary fields, human authority, revocation and fail-closed behaviour.
7. RED / AMBER / GREEN / HOLD is the common operational language where applicable; thresholds remain product-specific.
8. Revenue Rescue recovery algorithms remain protected and standalone.
9. Stock Sense inventory logic remains a distinct commercial SKU; NightOwl is a configuration.
10. Lens Saver is a real standalone Vision/Optical product and must remain visible in the portfolio.
11. Synchronicity/Tarot and Ancestor Journey are real standalone consumer/reflection products and must remain visible in the portfolio.
12. Historical lineage is preserved for provenance but cannot compete with canonical runtime routing.
13. GitHub + Neon + Cloudflare is the active platform standard. Do not reintroduce Vercel.
14. New ideas are classified as Shared Core capability, extension/configuration, family module, standalone product, incubator item or archive **before** a new repository is created.

# Completion definition

The ecosystem is not “clean” merely because repositories were renamed or archived. It is clean when every known product, capability, configuration, historical lineage and external/client asset has:
- a named family/home;
- a canonical/active/historical/planned/excluded status;
- a data/IP boundary;
- a reuse/link rule;
- no unexplained competing source of truth.

This register is the forward master map. The detailed historical 106-item register and repository family register remain supporting evidence rather than being discarded.
